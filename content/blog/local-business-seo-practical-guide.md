Local SEO is less about secret hacks and more about **clarity + credibility + consistency**.

If you serve Australian customers, start with the basics that never age badly.

## Fix the website fundamentals first

### Clear service definitions

Each core service deserves a plain explanation: what it includes, who it is for and what happens next.

### Internal linking

Link related services and proof pages so visitors—and search engines—understand what you want to be known for.

### Fast mobile UX

Most local discovery still happens on phones. Slow pages quietly kill enquiries.

## Google Business Profile is part of the stack

Keep your listing accurate:

- Categories that reflect what you actually sell
- Service areas that match real operations
- Photos updated periodically

Your website and GBP should tell the **same story**.

## FAQs are underrated (including for AI summaries)

Well-written FAQs often mirror how people ask questions out loud—especially for voice search phrasing.

Use short answers directly under the question, then expand if needed.

## Avoid classic traps

- Duplicate suburb pages with near-identical copy
- Keyword stuffing that reads robotic
- Promises like “#1 rankings” with no context
