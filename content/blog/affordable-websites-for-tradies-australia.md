Most tradie enquiries still happen on mobile—often while someone is on a job or waiting in a car. That means your website has one job first: **make trust and next steps obvious in under ten seconds**.

## The pages that actually matter

You do not need fifteen pages on day one. A strong tradie site usually nails:

- **Services** grouped the way homeowners search (not internal jargon).
- **Areas you serve**, described honestly (avoid spammy “suburb doorway” clones).
- **Proof**: recent jobs, short testimonials, licences/credentials where relevant.
- **A frictionless enquiry path**: tap-to-call, email and a short form.

If you run paid ads or seasonal offers, add a **landing page** later rather than bloating the homepage.

## What slows projects down (and how to avoid it)

The most common delay is not “design”—it is **content readiness**: photos, service wording and who answers enquiries once leads arrive.

Use a simple checklist:

1. List your top six services (what people actually ask for).
2. Gather ten decent site photos (before/after works well).
3. Decide what enquiry channel you want to prioritise (call vs form).

## Tradie SEO without gimmicks

Think “helpful clarity”, not tricks:

- Answer repeat customer questions as FAQs (great for AI summaries too).
- Keep headings descriptive (“Emergency electrician in Western Sydney” beats clever wordplay).
- Link related services internally so visitors (and crawlers) understand your strengths.

## When to keep scope intentionally small

If you are early-stage, ship a credible site fast, then iterate. Add pages when there is a real customer reason—not because a checklist says so.
