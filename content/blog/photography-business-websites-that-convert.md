Photography sites fail when they are beautiful but **unclear**. Visitors should understand what you shoot, who it is for and how to enquire—without hunting.

## Curate before you showcase

Your homepage is not the entire archive. Pick a tight sequence:

1. Signature hero work
2. Two or three speciality lanes (weddings, portraits, motorsport—whatever is real)
3. A calm enquiry prompt

## Navigation that respects creatives

Keep menus short. If you need depth, use category landing pages rather than mega-menus that overwhelm phones.

## Performance is part of the luxury feel

Slow galleries feel accidental. Lazy-load below-the-fold imagery and avoid uploading giant originals straight into pages.

## Trust cues that actually matter

Short testimonials with context (“elopement”, “brand shoot”) outperform vague superlatives.

Link out to Instagram thoughtfully—don’t send every visitor away instantly unless it is intentional.
